04 / TRANSFORMATION RULES, LEGEND & DATA CONTRACT

Four-stage transformation

L0 — Statute: immutable section anchor and legal-version reference. Statutory titles alone do not establish legal elements.

L1 — Police: source-specific assertions with document, passage, date, revision and source family.

L2 — Court-side: filings, submissions and judicial findings separated by role/status and linked to the same proposition IDs.

L3 — Interpretation: computationally detect shared addresses, source-only addresses, missing metadata and changes across revisions. Human reviewers determine actual agreement, contradiction, legal significance and admissibility.

Required import columns

section, layer, element, proposition, assertion, source, passage, observed, revision, group, status

Optional: id, source_hash, case_id, document_author, extraction_method, legal_version, reviewed_by, review_date
